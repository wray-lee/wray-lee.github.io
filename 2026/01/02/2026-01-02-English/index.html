    English

    所以

    普通 (Don’t use) 正式 (Standard) 高级 (Advanced/Specific) 适用场景
    So Therefore Consequently 强调后果/结果
    So Thus Hence 解释由来/逻辑推导
    So So that… Thereby + ing 强调手段与结果的紧密联系
    So In response Accordingly 强调根据情况采取相应行动

    Given 倒装

    thereby 借此 He signed the contract, thereby accepting the terms.

    因为

    普通 (Basic) 进阶 (Formal) 高阶/学术 (Advanced/Specific) 适用场景
    Because of (noun) Due to In light of / Given 强调背景、前提条件
    Because of (noun) Owing to By virtue of 强调凭借某种特性/优点
    Because (sentence) Since / As In that 强调具体的逻辑点 (The logic lies in…)
    Because (sentence) - …can be attributed to… 强调客观归因 (被动语态)
    Because (sentence) - Stem from / Arise from 强调根源/由来

    表明

    indicate

    confirm

    be consistent with

    provide

    clarify

    同时

    你的意图 普通 (Basic) 高级 (Advanced / Specific) 典型场景
    时间上的精准同步 At the same time Simultaneously 刺激呈现、瞬间反应
    时间段的重合 At the same time Concurrently 多任务处理、进程
    无干扰的平行进行 At the same time In parallel 多GPU训练、多组实验
    逻辑对比 (虽然…) But at the same time While / Whereas 比较模型优劣、对照组
    紧密配合/协同 Together In tandem / In conjunction 模块间的交互
    过渡到另一件事 At the same time Meanwhile / In the interim 汇报工作进度

    也/同样

    你的意图 普通 (Basic) 高级 (Advanced/Formal) 适用场景
    追加观点 (加法) Also / And Furthermore / Moreover 强调论据递进，更有说服力
    追加步骤 (补充) Also Additionally / In addition 描述实验步骤或额外工作
    类比 (A是, B也是) Me too / Also Likewise / Similarly 比较两个模型或两组数据
    罗列 (A和B也…) And also As well as / Along with 在一个句子里列举多个对象
    强调 (不仅A也B) … also … Not only… but… 强调双重优点或双重发现
