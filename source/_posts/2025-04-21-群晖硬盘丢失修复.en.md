# Synology Hard Drive Loss Repair

> Synology's system data is stored on the first and second partitions of each hard drive; the first is the operating system, and the second is swap.

## Mounting

Esxi mounts the vhdx of the raid disk onto the Linux system.

## Formatting

``` bash
# 重复n（硬盘数量）遍，根据挂载的硬盘更改X盘符
mkfs.btrfs /dev/sdX1 -f
mkfs.btrfs /dev/sdX2 -f
```
