# 清理wsl空间

1. 要找到wsl的vhd文件: `ext4.vhdx(%userprofile%\AppData\Local\Packages\22955VineelSai.ArchWSL_qz230bc1wsk9j\LocalState\)`
2. 打开PowerShell, 输入命令：

```text
wsl --shutdown
diskpart
# open window Diskpart
select vdisk file="D:\WSL-Arch\ext4.vhdx"
attach vdisk readonly
compact vdisk
detach vdisk
exit


```
