# OpenWrt Fingerprint Modification

After compiling it yourself, the fingerprint does not match the official one and needs to be replaced with the official fingerprint.
vi /usr/lib/opkg/status
Replace
5.10.100-1-68a2948d7c9d61b7fbc2eb39be16e450
with the official
5.10.107-1-6c49d71fca1b8e8ae0022be8b63be63f

opkg install kmod-usb-net-rtl8152 --force-depends
