| Special symbol | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| * (asterisk) | Represents any time. For example, the first "*" means executing once every minute in an hour. |
| , (comma) | Represents non-consecutive times. For example, "0 8,12,16***command" means executing the command once at 8:00, 12:00, and 16:00 every day. |
| - (hyphen) | Represents a continuous time range. For example, "0 5 ** 1-6 command" means executing the command at 5:00 AM from Monday to Saturday. |
