### Tunnel Relay

#### Method 1:

Relay server: `./gost -L tcp://:9527 -F relay+tls://1.1.1.1:8443`

Relay server: `./gost -L tcp://:9527/1.1.1.1:80 -F relay+tls://1.1.1.1:8443`
