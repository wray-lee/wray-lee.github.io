In fact, this takes advantage of the VPN domain being the parent domain of the intranet domain, accessing the second-level domain through the first-level domain.

Second-level domains are composed of letters (A–Z, a–z, upper and lower case, etc.), digits (0–9), and hyphens (-). Domain levels are separated by dots (.). The length of a second-level domain cannot exceed 20 characters. Unless there are special reasons, it is recommended to use the applicant's English name (or abbreviation) or Hanyu Pinyin name (or abbreviation) as the second-level domain to keep the domain name clear and concise.
