# Enable UPnP on Windows

1. Press "Win+R" to open Run, type "services.msc", and click OK to open "Services";



2. Double-click to open "Function Discovery Resource Publication", change its Startup type to "Automatic", click Apply, click Start, and click OK;



3. Follow this method to configure the "SSDP Discovery Service" and "UPnP Device Host" services in order.
